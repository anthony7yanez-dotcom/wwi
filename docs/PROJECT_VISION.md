# PROJECT VISION — DARK FANTASY TURN-BASED RPG

## 1. Your Role

You are my lead game developer, game designer, creative director, and technical collaborator.

Your objective is to help me create a complete, immersive, story-driven, turn-based fantasy RPG inspired by the gameplay philosophy of **Golden Sun**.

I want you to understand my creative vision before making major design or development decisions.

**IMPORTANT: We have already created concept characters and visual artwork for this project. These existing assets are foundational to the game's identity. Do not discard, replace, or unnecessarily redesign them. Inspect the existing project and reuse the established characters, environments, artwork, and other assets wherever possible. New visual designs should complement the established artistic direction.**

This game should feel like a fully realized adventure, not a generic RPG template.

---

## 2. Core Game Concept

**Genre:** Dark Fantasy / Story-Driven RPG / Turn-Based Combat / Overworld Exploration

**Primary Gameplay Inspiration:** Golden Sun

**Atmosphere:** Dark, gloomy, melancholic, mysterious, oppressive, and occasionally beautiful.

**Central Premise:**

The world is dying.

An ancient, sacred flame that has sustained the existence of the world for countless generations is slowly fading.

As its light diminishes, darkness spreads across the land. Kingdoms crumble, creatures become corrupted, ancient evils awaken, and the natural balance of the world begins to collapse.

The player takes control of a character who becomes responsible for rekindling this dying flame before its final light disappears.

Should the flame be extinguished, the world as it is known will cease to exist.

However, not everyone wishes to save it.

A mysterious cult worships the coming darkness. Its followers believe the destruction of the existing world will give birth to a new reality shaped by suffering, darkness, and malevolent powers.

These cultists perform forbidden rituals, summon terrifying creatures, corrupt entire regions, and actively hunt those attempting to restore the flame.

The player's journey becomes a race against an approaching apocalypse.

The deeper the player ventures into the world, the more they discover about the flame, its origins, the cult, and the true consequences of rekindling it.

I want the story to feature mystery, meaningful discoveries, tragedy, hope, memorable characters, and potentially difficult moral choices.

Do not assume the player knows everything about the world at the beginning. Reveal its history naturally through exploration, dialogue, ancient ruins, quests, and important encounters.

---

## 3. Exploration and Overworld Gameplay

The exploration experience should closely resemble the fundamental design philosophy of Golden Sun.

The player directly controls a character who walks through an interconnected fantasy world.

The world should contain:

- Towns, villages, castles, ruins, temples, forests, caves, mountains, dungeons, and forgotten locations.
- NPCs with meaningful dialogue, quests, personal stories, and information.
- Interactive objects, treasure chests, hidden passages, switches, environmental puzzles, and secrets.
- Equipment, weapons, armor, upgrades, relics, and rare artifacts to discover.
- Hostile enemies and powerful bosses encountered throughout the adventure.
- Areas that require exploration abilities, acquired skills, or story progression to access.
- Optional locations that reward curiosity and careful exploration.

Movement and interactions should feel deliberate, responsive, and polished.

The player should be encouraged to explore their surroundings rather than simply follow objective markers.

**Exploration abilities should matter.** Similar to the way Golden Sun incorporates supernatural abilities into puzzles, I want certain character abilities to interact directly with the environment.

For example, a learned ability might move an obstacle, reveal hidden objects, manipulate an ancient mechanism, or unlock an inaccessible pathway.

These mechanics should fit our world's lore and character classes rather than directly copying Golden Sun's abilities.

The world should feel interconnected and gradually open as the player's abilities and knowledge expand.

---

## 4. Character Creation and Class Selection

During the game's introduction, the player will choose their character's starting class.

This should be a meaningful decision that influences their gameplay experience.

Classes should differ in:

- Starting attributes and combat capabilities.
- Available weapons and equipment.
- Special abilities and combat techniques.
- Magical or physical strengths.
- Long-term progression and upgrades.
- Distinct tactical approaches.

We will develop the actual class roster together, keeping established character concepts and artwork in mind.

The character should grow stronger through exploration, battles, discoveries, and progression.

New moves, abilities, equipment, relics, and upgrades should become available throughout the game.

A class should feel increasingly powerful as the player develops it, but each class should retain unique strengths and weaknesses.

Do not arbitrarily introduce class switching or additional playable party members without first discussing how they fit the intended design.

---

## 5. Turn-Based Battle System

**Golden Sun is the primary inspiration for the combat experience.**

Exploration should transition into a dedicated turn-based battle encounter.

Combat should feel strategic, visually engaging, and satisfying.

### Core Combat Features

- Traditional turn-based encounters.
- Player commands such as Attack, Abilities, Items, Defend, and other class-specific actions.
- Health, resource management, attributes, and status effects.
- Enemy strengths, weaknesses, resistances, and combat behaviors.
- Physical and magical attacks.
- Unique class abilities.
- Equipment and relics that meaningfully modify character performance.
- Distinct enemy types with varied strategies.
- Challenging boss encounters with unique mechanics and multiple attack patterns.

Combat should become progressively more tactical as new abilities and enemies are introduced.

### Combat Presentation

I want combat to have the cinematic yet readable presentation associated with classic turn-based RPGs.

Attacks should feel impactful.

Powerful abilities should have distinct animations and visual effects.

Enemies should be visually intimidating when appropriate, especially bosses and creatures summoned by the cult.

Combat must remain understandable and enjoyable rather than becoming excessively complex.

We should develop original mechanics that give this game its own distinct identity.

---

## 6. Equipment, Relics, and Progression

Progression should be a major part of the experience.

The player should consistently discover opportunities to strengthen and customize their character.

### Equipment

Weapons, armor, accessories, and special equipment should provide meaningful statistical or mechanical advantages.

Some equipment may possess unique effects.

### Abilities

New combat and exploration abilities can be learned through progression, quests, ancient knowledge, relics, or other appropriate systems.

### Relics

Relics should be particularly exciting discoveries.

They may provide powerful passive effects, alter abilities, unlock unique interactions, or enable unconventional character builds.

Relics should have a meaningful connection to the world's ancient history and dying flame.

### Upgrades

Introduce a thoughtful system for upgrading equipment or abilities as appropriate to the setting.

Avoid excessive grinding or meaningless numerical increases.

The player should feel rewarded for exploring, defeating powerful enemies, completing quests, and discovering hidden locations.

---

## 7. Enemy and Boss Design

The world should contain a diverse selection of dangerous enemies.

Creatures should reflect the regions they inhabit and the encroaching darkness.

Enemies might include corrupted wildlife, monstrous beings, ancient guardians, cursed warriors, summoned entities, and cult followers.

Boss encounters should be memorable milestones.

Each major boss should have:

- A distinct appearance and personality or narrative significance.
- Unique combat abilities and mechanics.
- A clear thematic connection to its location or role.
- Interesting challenges that reward tactical thinking.
- Meaningful rewards or story progression.

Cultist leaders and their summoned abominations should serve as important antagonists.

Not every dangerous creature needs to belong to the cult. The world should contain its own ancient threats, mysteries, and hostile inhabitants.

---

## 8. The Dying Flame — Central World Mechanic

The dying flame should be the central symbol of the game.

Its deterioration should influence the world, its atmosphere, and the story.

As its power declines, the player may witness changes such as:

- Settlements becoming abandoned.
- Landscapes falling into decay.
- Darkness consuming once-safe areas.
- Increasingly dangerous creatures appearing.
- NPCs losing hope or becoming desperate.
- Ancient forces growing stronger.

I want the player to feel that the world is genuinely approaching its end.

However, do not implement a strict real-time countdown that unfairly punishes exploration unless we explicitly decide to adopt one.

Instead, initially explore a narrative or progression-based system where the worsening condition of the world can be represented through significant story events and player decisions.

The flame should also carry mystery. Its history, purpose, and the consequences of restoring it should gradually become more complicated as the player uncovers the truth.

---

## 9. World Atmosphere and Artistic Direction

The game should feel consistently dark and atmospheric.

Visual inspiration should include:

- Dying embers and fading firelight.
- Ancient, weathered architecture.
- Forgotten kingdoms.
- Mist-covered forests.
- Crumbling stone ruins.
- Abandoned settlements.
- Oppressive dungeons.
- Unsettling supernatural environments.
- Beautiful but haunting landscapes.

Lighting should play an important role.

Warm firelight should contrast with the cold, decaying world.

The game should evoke isolation, tension, wonder, melancholy, and the occasional sense of hope.

**Visual consistency is extremely important.**

Use the existing concept characters and visual assets as the foundation for the game's presentation.

Do not introduce an unrelated art style.

New characters, environments, UI elements, and enemies should feel like they belong to the same universe.

The visual experience should support the gameplay, with readable characters, clear interactive elements, and cohesive battle and exploration environments.

---

## 10. Storytelling and Worldbuilding

I want a world with depth rather than a straightforward sequence of battles.

Develop meaningful lore surrounding:

- The origin of the sacred flame.
- The civilization or beings responsible for its existence.
- The reason it is dying.
- The rise of the cult.
- The forces behind the summoning rituals.
- The history of the world's kingdoms and civilizations.
- The meaning and consequences of rekindling the flame.

NPC dialogue should reveal history, culture, conflict, and personal struggles.

Some characters should have individual motivations unrelated to the central quest.

Include optional stories, hidden lore, and discoveries that reward players who investigate their surroundings.

Avoid overwhelming the player with exposition. Let the world communicate its history visually and through gameplay.

The story should be mature and emotionally compelling without relying exclusively on shock value or excessive violence.

---

## 11. User Interface and Player Experience

The game should include the fundamental features expected from a polished RPG.

These include:

- Main menu.
- Character and class information.
- Inventory management.
- Equipment screen.
- Ability and progression menus.
- Relic management.
- Quest journal.
- Dialogue interface.
- Battle command interface.
- Saving and loading.
- Clear information about character attributes and combat effects.

The interface should complement the dark fantasy atmosphere while remaining readable and intuitive.

The player should not have to struggle with the UI to enjoy the game.

## 13. Development Strategy

I want this project developed systematically rather than attempting to create the entire game in a single step.

First, understand the vision and inspect the existing work.

Then organize development into manageable stages.

**Suggested development priorities:**

**Stage 1 — Project Assessment**
Review the existing files, characters, concept artwork, technology, and development progress.

**Stage 2 — Core Exploration**
Establish functional player movement, world navigation, environmental interactions, and NPC dialogue.

**Stage 3 — Combat Prototype**
Implement a functional turn-based battle system with basic abilities, enemies, and battle transitions.

**Stage 4 — Character Progression**
Introduce the class-selection system, equipment, abilities, inventory, and relic mechanics.

**Stage 5 — First Playable Region**
Develop an atmospheric starting location with interactive objects, NPCs, enemies, exploration puzzles, and a story objective.

**Stage 6 — First Major Boss**
Create a complete dungeon or equivalent story location culminating in a memorable boss encounter.

**Stage 7 — Expansion**
Develop additional locations, enemy types, quests, progression mechanics, and story chapters.

**Stage 8 — Polish**
Improve animation, sound, interface, balance, transitions, atmosphere, performance, and overall player experience.

Prioritize making each system functional and enjoyable before expanding its scale.

The first major milestone should be a polished, playable vertical slice that demonstrates exploration, dialogue, environmental interaction, combat, progression, and the intended atmosphere.
